How it works

  1. Have the tokens on Ethereum. If they're on Shibarium, Base or another network, move them to Ethereum first with the official Shib bridge.
  2. Burn once a week or month. Each burn is one Ethereum transaction, so burning in batches keeps network fees low.
  3. Anyone can check it. Burns go to 0x…dEaD on Ethereum, the address every burn tracker counts.

Payments taken on Ethereum already burned their share in the payment. You only need this for the other networks.
