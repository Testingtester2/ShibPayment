How it works

  1. Burn from where the tokens are. On Ethereum it's a plain burn. On Base, Polygon, BNB Chain and 8 other networks, Chainlink's official bridge carries them to the burn address on Ethereum. Burn from every network
  2. Burn once a week or month. Every burn costs a network fee, so burning in batches keeps fees low.
  3. Anyone can check it. Burns go to 0x…dEaD on Ethereum, the address every burn tracker counts.

Payments taken on Ethereum already burned their share in the payment. You only need this for the other networks.

Burn from every network

Finds your SHIB, BONE and LEASH on Ethereum and 11 other networks, and burns what you choose. Off Ethereum, Chainlink's official bridge (CCIP) carries each token to 0x…dEaD on Ethereum, so the burn counts. You confirm each network in your wallet (one tap in wallets that bundle steps, like MetaMask), and pay a small bridge fee in that network's own coin.

Not covered: Shibarium (including TREAT there and BONE gas) and SHIB on Solana aren't on Chainlink's bridge. Move those to Ethereum first (Shibarium bridge, or Sunrise for Solana), then burn them at the top of this page.
