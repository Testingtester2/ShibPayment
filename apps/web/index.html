How it works

STEP 1

Paste your wallet

Your ShibWallet address or your .shib name. That's where the money goes.

STEP 2

Set a price

In your own money: dollars, euros, naira, rupees and more. We convert it at checkout.

STEP 3

Share it

Send the link in a chat, print the QR at your stall, or paste one button into your website.

For websites

One script tag and one element. Works in any site builder that allows custom HTML. Your server confirms each payment on-chain before shipping.

ShibariumEthereumBNB ChainArbitrumOP MainnetBasePolygonScrollLineaCeloGnosisMantleAvalanche 
<script src="https://YOUR-SHIBPAYMENT-SITE/shib-pay.js"></script>

<shib-pay
  to="0xYourWalletAddress"
  amount="12.50" currency="USD"
  burn="2" order="A-1017"
  label="Granny's Bakery">
</shib-pay>

<script>
  document.addEventListener('shibpay:paid', (e) => {
    // Send e.detail.txHash to your server, which calls
    // verifyPayment() from @shibpay/core before shipping.
  });
</script>
